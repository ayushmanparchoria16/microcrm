User Data Deletion Instructions

Effective Date: October 2026 • Compliant with Meta Platform Terms

At MicroCRM (Parchhoria Technologies), we respect your privacy and provide transparent, effortless controls over your personal data. If you have interacted with any WhatsApp Business account, digital storefront, or lead inquiry system powered by MicroCRM, you have the right to request the complete deletion of your data.

How to Request Data Deletion

You can choose any of the three options below to request immediate deletion of your records:

Option 1: Direct WhatsApp Command (Instant)

Open your WhatsApp chat with the business number and send any of the following messages: STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, or DELETE MY DATA. The AI conversational agent will immediately deactivate automated outreach and flag your contact record for erasure.

Option 2: Email Data Erasure Request

Send an email to our Data Protection Officer at support@microcrm.site or ayushman@microcrm.site with:

  • Subject: Meta Platform User Data Deletion Request
  • Body: Your phone number with country code (e.g., +91 98765 43210) and the business name you contacted.

Option 3: Merchant Console Removal

If you are a merchant using MicroCRM, you can permanently erase any customer or lead record directly from your dashboard by navigating to Contacts → Customer Drawer → Delete Customer. This permanently purges their chat history, profile, and appointments.

What Data Is Deleted?

Upon processing your deletion request, the following records are permanently expunged:

  • Your phone number, contact name, and WhatsApp user identifiers (JID / phone number ID).
  • Full conversation history, inbound questions, and AI-generated responses.
  • Any associated customer tags, notes, or appointment booking records.

Deletion Timeline & Confirmation

Verified deletion requests are processed within 48 business hours. Once deleted, you will receive a confirmation email or acknowledgment that your records have been completely purged from our active databases and backup routines.

Questions & Assistance

If you have any questions regarding your personal data or this procedure, contact our Grievance Officer at ayushman@microcrm.site or via WhatsApp at +91 87704 66442.
