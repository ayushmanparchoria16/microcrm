Enterprise Cloud Multi-Tenancy

MicroCRM operates on high-availability cloud infrastructure hosted at https://app.microcrm.site with zero client app install required. Everything is engineered around strict parameter-scoped multi-tenancy:

  • Strict Tenant Scoping: Every query executed in the PostgreSQL database enforces WHERE business_id = %s, guaranteeing that competitors and other businesses can never view or leak each other's data.
  • Dedicated Slug Routing: Each merchant receives an instant custom link (e.g. app.microcrm.site/book/your-business) which immediately maps to their business profile, staff members, catalog services, and inventory items.
  • 24/7 Cloud Availability: Managed SSL encryption, automated HTTP/2 transport, and 99.9% uptime SLA ensure customers can book services or place orders at any hour of the day.

DPDPA 2023 & Anti-Theft Protection

Data security is baked into the foundation of MicroCRM to comply with national privacy regulations and protect merchant business assets:

Anti-Theft Phone Number Masking

Staff data theft is a major risk for sales agencies and salons. MicroCRM automatically masks customer contact numbers (e.g., +91 62641*****) for sales reps and non-admin employees. Employees can chat with leads on WhatsApp Web directly without gaining access to download or copy raw customer lists.

  • Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA 2023): Strict purpose limitation ensures customer data collected via booking forms or WhatsApp is used only for service fulfillment.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Granular permissions separate Business Owners/Admins, Store Managers, and Sales Representatives. Full raw exports and billing reports are restricted to administrators.

Mobile-First Digital Storefront & Catalog

Turn search queries from Google Maps and Instagram directly into booked sales. Every MicroCRM merchant gets a lightning-fast, progressive web storefront accessible on any smartphone:

  • Zero App Install: Customers tap your link and immediately browse products, services, diagnostic repair tiers, or plot plans without needing to install anything from an app store.
  • Instagram Reel Video Proof: Embed your top Instagram styling transformations or repair demonstrations directly into service cards. Customers tap once to watch authentic work proofs.
  • 1-Click WhatsApp Booking: Selected services and cart items are formatted into a structured WhatsApp message, automatically notifying your staff and populating the CRM lead drawer.
  • Interactive Layout Maps: For real estate builders, buyers can inspect interactive master plans (e.g. Aahana Residency), tap specific plots to see square footage, facing, and status.

24/7 WhatsApp AI Sales Receptionist

Our background AI sales agent connects directly to your business WhatsApp number using WhatsApp Web JS technology. It operates around the clock without extra personnel:

  • Instant Automated Replies: The AI answers customer inquiries instantly regarding business hours, service prices, address/Google Maps directions, and appointment availability.
  • Smart PDF Invoice Delivery: When your counter staff creates a bill in the CRM, the WhatsApp AI automatically sends a branded PDF tax receipt directly to the customer's phone.
  • Automated Reminder Dispatch: Scheduled background workers automatically send appointment reminders to customers 24 hours prior to their booking, slashing no-shows by up to 70%.

Smart POS Billing & Khata Ledger

A fast point-of-sale interface built for retail counters, repair desks, and salons:

  • Barcode & SKU Scanner: Fast product lookup with barcode scanner support, real-time stock deductions, and low-inventory warnings.
  • GST & Non-GST Invoicing: Generate compliant invoices with customizable tax breakdowns, discounts, and payment mode tracking (UPI, Cash, Card, Khata).
  • Customer Khata (Credit Ledger): Keep track of outstanding balances, send payment reminder links on WhatsApp, and reconcile payments seamlessly.
