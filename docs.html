MicroCRM Documentation

Welcome to the official documentation for MicroCRM. This guide covers all the core features of the platform and how it empowers modern businesses with 24/7 automated WhatsApp AI sales agents, pre-sales pipelines, and multi-tenant security.

Enterprise Cloud & Hybrid Architecture

MicroCRM provides an enterprise-grade cloud SaaS platform (app.microcrm.site) equipped with high-availability 24/7 cloud infrastructure alongside optional local desktop deployment:

  • 24/7 Cloud SaaS Availability: Access your CRM dashboard, live WhatsApp Web inbox, and lead drawers anytime from any web browser or smartphone.
  • Multi-Tenant PostgreSQL Backend: Engineered for speed, stability, and scale with parameter-scoped multi-tenant database isolation (WHERE business_id = %s).
  • Hybrid Desktop & Local Mode: Supports local PC desktop deployment with SQLite for standalone businesses needing on-premise operation.

Security, DPDPA 2023 & Anti-Theft Protection

MicroCRM incorporates multi-layered enterprise security safeguards to protect customer data privacy and safeguard lead databases against theft.

  • 🛡️ DPDPA 2023 Compliance: Built in accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023. Restricts automated messaging to explicit consent and purpose limitation.
  • 🔐 100% Multi-Tenant Data Isolation: Every database query is strictly scoped by WHERE business_id = %s, ensuring competitor businesses or different franchises can never access or leak each other's leads.
  • 🙈 Anti-Theft Phone Number Masking: Non-admin sales reps view masked phone numbers (+91 62641*****) in lead drawers, reports, and WhatsApp Web inboxes to prevent staff database theft.

Smart WhatsApp AI Assistant

We've integrated a powerful AI agent directly into your business WhatsApp number using WhatsApp Web JS technology. It operates entirely in the background.

  • 24/7 Customer Support: The AI reads incoming messages and intelligently answers questions about your business, operating hours, and product catalog.
  • Automated PDF Billing: Whenever you generate a sale in the CRM, the WhatsApp AI automatically sends a beautifully formatted PDF receipt directly to the customer's WhatsApp.
  • Appointment Reminders: The system schedules background workers that automatically send WhatsApp reminders to customers 24 hours before their scheduled appointment.

Smart Call Assistant

Never miss a customer inquiry when your shop is busy. The Smart Call Assistant acts as a digital receptionist.

  • Voice AI Integration: Integrates with voice providers to answer calls intelligently, speaking in natural language to your customers.
  • Auto-Booking: The AI can take appointment requests over the phone and log them directly into your CRM calendar without human intervention.

Free Client Web Portal

Every MicroCRM installation automatically hosts a beautiful, responsive web portal for your customers.

  • Online Storefront: Customers can browse your synchronized inventory catalogue online.
  • Self-Service Booking: Allow customers to select available time slots and book services directly from their smartphones.
  • Secure Access: Customers log in using OTP authentication to view their past bills and active appointments.

Real Estate & Pre-Sales CRM

Built specifically for real estate developers, agencies, and sales teams to manage property listings, buyer leads, and site visit tours automatically.

  • Automated Lead Capture: Incoming WhatsApp messages automatically create new lead records in the Pre-Sales pipeline with status tracking (New Lead, Contacted, Visit Scheduled, Interested, Converted).
  • WhatsApp AI Property Selling Agent: The AI shares project details, available plots/flats/villas, base prices, downloadable PDF brochure links, and website portal links directly in customer chat.
  • Dedicated Rep Lead Attribution: Incoming leads received on an employee or admin's linked WhatsApp number are automatically assigned directly to that specific sales representative.
  • Follow-Up Date Tracking: Staff members can update lead notes, change lead status, or schedule next follow-up dates directly via WhatsApp.

Complete CRM & POS

The core of MicroCRM is a full-featured administration dashboard accessible via localhost on your machine.

  • Fast Billing & POS: A streamlined point-of-sale interface for quickly checking out customers in-store.
  • Inventory Tracking: Keep track of stock levels, set low-stock alerts, and manage product variations.
  • Staff & HR: Manage employee attendance, assign daily tasks, and track performance.
  • Analytics Dashboard: View visual reports on your daily sales, popular products, and customer growth.

Data Export & Cloud Backups

MicroCRM provides 1-click Data Export capabilities built directly into every module alongside automated cloud database backups.

  • 1-Click Module CSV Exports: Business owners can export their company's leads, properties, and analytics reports to clean CSV files directly from the Pre-Sales Leads, Properties, and Reports tabs.
  • Automated Cloud Backups: System database snapshots and infrastructure backups are automatically managed 24/7 on the cloud server backend to ensure zero data loss.
