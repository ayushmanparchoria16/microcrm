Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

At MicroCRM, we believe your business data belongs strictly to you. Our architecture is designed from the ground up around Zero-Cloud Local Storage, ensuring complete privacy, control, and data sovereignty.

1. Zero-Cloud Data Principle

MicroCRM runs locally on your Windows computer. All customer information, contact details, invoices, inventory stock, staff attendance, and chat histories are stored inside a local SQLite database (`app.db`) on your physical machine.

  • We do not host or store your business transactions on central third-party cloud database servers.
  • Your financial data, customer lists, and business notes never leave your personal computer without your explicit direction.

2. WhatsApp Integration Privacy

MicroCRM utilizes WhatsApp Web JS to connect your business WhatsApp account directly to your local instance:

  • WhatsApp authentication sessions are saved locally inside your installation directory (`whatsapp-gateway\.wwebjs_auth`).
  • Messages processed by your local AI assistant are handled on-device to deliver auto-replies, generate PDF invoices, and send appointment reminders.
  • We do not sell, harvest, or transmit your customers' phone numbers or chat conversations to external telemetry servers.

3. Secure Tunneling & Client Portal

When you enable the Client Web Portal for online bookings or digital store links (e.g., `yourbusiness.microcrm.site`), MicroCRM creates an encrypted Cloudflare Tunnel connection:

  • The tunnel acts as a secure pass-through bridge directly from the internet to your local PC.
  • No customer data is stored on intermediary hosting servers. Request traffic flows directly to your local application engine.

4. Automated Backups & Data Ownership

MicroCRM includes built-in automated 1-click rolling backups stored inside your local `backups/` folder. You retain 100% ownership of your backup `.db` files and can export, move, or delete them at any time.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding privacy or data handling, feel free to reach out to our team at Contact Support or email us at support@microcrm.site.
