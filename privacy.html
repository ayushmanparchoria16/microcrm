Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Effective Date: October 2026

Data Sovereignty & Isolation Promise

At MicroCRM (developed by Parchhoria Technologies), we operate on a core foundation: Your business data belongs exclusively to you. We enforce strict multi-tenant database isolation (WHERE business_id = %s), full compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA 2023), and staff anti-theft phone masking. We never sell, monetize, or harvest your customer records.

1. Scope & Service Applications

This Privacy Policy applies to all personal data and business information processed through:

  • The MicroCRM Multi-Tenant Cloud Platform (https://app.microcrm.site)
  • Merchant digital storefronts and self-service booking portals (https://app.microcrm.site/book/[slug] and microcrm.site)
  • Automated 24/7 WhatsApp AI sales assistants, billing dispatchers, and appointment reminder engines
  • Associated point-of-sale (POS) billing, inventory tracking, and client management software modules

2. DPDPA 2023 Compliance & Lawful Basis of Processing

In adherence to India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA 2023) and applicable global data privacy standards, MicroCRM processes customer information strictly under recognized lawful bases:

  • Purpose Limitation & Consent: Customer information (such as customer name, WhatsApp contact number, vehicle/device details for repairs, bridal booking dates, or real estate plot inquiries) is collected solely for explicit fulfillment of the merchant's business services.
  • No Telemetry Tracking or Ad Mining: We do not track users across the web, inject tracking cookies from third-party advertising networks, or monetize customer purchasing habits.
  • Notice at Collection: When a consumer uses a public storefront (such as Rashmi R Makeup or Shri Ranawat Infotech) to book an appointment or place an order via WhatsApp, they voluntarily initiate contact and are presented with transparent booking details.

3. 100% Multi-Tenant Data Isolation Guarantee

MicroCRM powers thousands of business transactions across diverse verticals—including beauty salons, computer repair centers, real estate developers, and retail stores. To safeguard operational integrity:

  • Strict Parameter-Scoped Tenancy: Every single database read, write, and analytical aggregation is strictly scoped by WHERE business_id = %s at the query execution level.
  • Zero Cross-Tenant Leakage: A merchant or franchise owner can never view, query, access, or export another business tenant's catalog, customer numbers, revenue totals, or sales leads.
  • Logical & Cryptographic Separation: Session tokens and authentication cookies are cryptographically signed with tenant-specific claims.

4. Anti-Theft Phone Number Masking for Sales Reps

Customer phone numbers are one of the most valuable assets of any business. To eliminate internal data theft or unauthorized employee poaching:

  • Role-Based Masking: Non-admin employees, sales staff, and support agents only see partially masked contact numbers (e.g. +91 62641*****) across the CRM lead drawers, customer lists, and report views.
  • Direct In-App WhatsApp Web Action: Employees can initiate WhatsApp conversations with leads directly through authorized action buttons without exposing the raw mobile number for copy-pasting or offline export.
  • Admin Visibility Control: Full unmasked numbers and raw data exports are strictly restricted to verified business owner / administrator roles.

5. WhatsApp Business & AI Assistant Privacy

MicroCRM integrates an intelligent conversational AI agent directly with your business WhatsApp number using secure WhatsApp Web session protocols:

  • On-Device / Private Gateway Sessions: WhatsApp session credentials (.wwebjs_auth) are encrypted and isolated within your dedicated tenant container.
  • Operational Automation: The AI processes messages solely to provide instant business answers (operating hours, service menu, catalog pricing), send automated PDF tax invoices, and schedule calendar appointments.
  • Zero External Data Sharing: Your customers' personal phone numbers and private chats are never transmitted to third parties, data aggregators, or marketing networks.

6. Data Security, Transport & Cloud Infrastructure

Our cloud infrastructure is hardened with industry-standard cybersecurity defenses:

  • TLS 1.3 Transport Encryption: All communications between web browsers, public mobile storefronts, and our backend servers are encrypted using high-grade TLS 1.3 SSL protocols.
  • Automated Cloud Backups: Cloud databases undergo daily automated snapshots to prevent data loss against hardware or infrastructure disruptions.
  • Data Portability & Zero Vendor Lock-In: Business owners can export clean CSV backups of their customers, leads, sales invoices, and catalog at any time with a single click.

7. Data Principal Rights (Access, Correction & Deletion)

Under the DPDPA 2023, data principals (both merchants and end-customers) have enforceable rights:

  • Right of Access & Summary: You may request an itemized copy of the personal data held within your tenant account.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: Customers can request the correction of inaccurate contact details or the complete erasure of their customer record by contacting the merchant or our Data Officer.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: We maintain a designated grievance redressal mechanism to respond to data inquiries within the statutory timelines.
